Our Story

A Legacy Built on Discretion and Depth

Maison was born from a simple conviction: that the finest properties deserve the most considered counsel. Our founder, Catherine Holloway, opened the original Mayfair office in 1994 with a small portfolio of exceptional homes and an unwavering belief in the power of personal service.

Over three decades, that conviction has taken us to twelve cities across four continents. We remain as selective about who we represent as we are about the properties we bring to market — because extraordinary homes deserve extraordinary stewardship.

Today, Maison stands as one of the most trusted names in luxury real estate, recognised not for scale, but for the calibre of our relationships and the rarity of our portfolio.
